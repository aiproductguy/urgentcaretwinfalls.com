When seeking laceration repair at an urgent care, expect the medical professional to thoroughly examine the wound, clean it thoroughly, and depending on the severity, close it with sutures, staples, or skin adhesive, while also providing local anesthetic for pain relief; they will likely give instructions on how to care for the wound at home, including checking for signs of infection and scheduling a follow-up appointment for suture removal if necessary; if the laceration is deep or involves tendons, nerves, or major blood vessels, further evaluation or referral to a specialist may be required. 
Key points about laceration repair at urgent care:
Assessment:
The doctor will evaluate the wound's depth, location, and potential damage to underlying structures. 
Cleaning:
The wound will be thoroughly cleaned with sterile solutions to remove debris. 
Anesthesia:
Local anesthetic will be injected to numb the area before repair. 
Closure methods:
Depending on the wound, the doctor may use sutures, staples, or tissue adhesive to close the laceration. 
Post-care instructions:
You will receive detailed instructions on how to keep the wound clean, change dressings, and monitor for signs of infection. 
When to go to an urgent care for a laceration:
A cut that is deep, gaping, or bleeding profusely 
Lacerations over joints 
Cuts on the face or hands
