A Department of Transportation (DOT) physical is required for individuals who drive commercial vehicles to ensure they are physically, mentally, and emotionally fit. The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) mandates this exam to ensure public safety.

Who Needs a DOT Physical?
A DOT physical is required for drivers of:

Vehicles carrying over 15 people.
Vehicles designed to carry over 8 people for pay.
Vehicles carrying hazardous materials that need a placard.
Vehicles weighing 10,000 pounds or more.
How to Obtain a DOT Medical Card
After passing the DOT physical, you receive a Medical Examiner's Certificate (MEC) valid for 2 years, unless you have conditions like diabetes or high blood pressure, which require annual checks.

What Does a DOT Physical Include?
The exam includes a medical history review and physical checks of:

Height, weight, blood pressure, vision, hearing, heart, lungs, abdomen, and more.
Urinalysis to detect potential health issues.
Additional tests may be required based on symptoms, including a sleep apnea test for relevant signs.
Where to Get a DOT Physical
Only certified medical professionals, such as MDs, DOs, PAs, or NPs, can perform DOT physicals. These professionals can be found through the FMCSA website.

How to Prepare
Preparation includes maintaining health, updating medications, ensuring glasses/contact lenses are up-to-date, avoiding alcohol, smoking, and managing weight.

What to Bring
Bring relevant medical records, including doctor contacts, medication details, blood sugar levels, and heart tests.

Disqualifying Conditions
Conditions like uncontrolled heart disease, epilepsy, diabetes, or severe vision and hearing loss can disqualify you. Some conditions may be reconsidered once managed, and exemptions can be requested from the FMCSA.

New DOT Physical Requirements
Vision: 20/40 vision in each eye with or without correction, and a 70-degree field of vision.
Hearing: Ability to hear a "forced whisper" from 5 feet away.
Blood Pressure: Below 140/90 to pass; higher readings require frequent checks or treatment.

Cost
DOT physicals generally cost between $85-$150, and some employers may cover the expense.
