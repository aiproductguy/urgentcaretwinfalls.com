An urgent care can help with nebulizer (neb) treatments for asthma by providing immediate access to a medical professional who can assess your symptoms, administer a nebulizer treatment with a medication like albuterol to quickly open your airways, and manage your asthma attack if necessary, offering relief through the mist inhaled directly into your lungs, especially when your symptoms are severe or you can't effectively use an inhaler on your own. 
Key points about urgent care and neb treatments for asthma:
Quick relief:
Urgent care provides fast access to a nebulizer treatment which can significantly improve breathing within minutes during an asthma attack. 
Medication administration:
A healthcare provider at urgent care can determine the appropriate medication dosage and administer the nebulizer treatment properly. 
Monitoring and assessment:
They will monitor your vital signs, including oxygen levels, and assess the severity of your asthma symptoms before and after the treatment. 
Follow-up care:
Depending on your situation, the urgent care provider may prescribe additional medications like oral steroids to help manage inflammation and prevent future asthma attacks.
