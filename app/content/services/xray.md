# X-ray & Imaging Services at Urgent Care of Twin Falls

Our facility offers state-of-the-art X-ray and imaging services to provide quick, accurate diagnoses for injuries and conditions. Our on-site imaging capabilities allow for immediate evaluation and treatment planning.

## Available Imaging Services

- Digital X-rays
- Diagnostic imaging
- Bone density scans
- Joint imaging
- Chest X-rays
- Extremity imaging
- Sports injury evaluation

## Common Conditions We Diagnose

- Fractures and breaks
- Sprains and strains
- Joint problems
- Chest conditions
- Bone abnormalities
- Sports injuries
- Work-related injuries

## Our Technology Advantage

1. **Digital Imaging**: We use advanced digital X-ray technology for:
   - Higher quality images
   - Lower radiation exposure
   - Faster results
   - Easy image sharing with specialists

2. **Immediate Results**: Our digital system provides instant image review for quick diagnosis.

3. **Expert Analysis**: Our trained medical professionals interpret results on-site.

## The Process

1. **Initial Assessment**: We determine if imaging is necessary for your condition.
2. **Quick Imaging**: Most X-rays take only minutes to complete.
3. **Immediate Review**: A medical provider reviews your images right away.
4. **Treatment Planning**: We develop your treatment plan based on the results.

## Safety and Comfort

- Minimal radiation exposure
- Comfortable positioning
- Quick procedure times
- Professional technicians
- Clean, modern equipment

## Insurance and Records

We work with most insurance providers and can easily share your imaging results with your primary care physician or specialist. Digital copies of your images are available upon request.
