# Illness Treatment at Urgent Care of Twin Falls

Our experienced medical team provides comprehensive care for a wide range of non-emergency illnesses. We offer prompt, effective treatment to help you feel better faster, without the long wait times of an emergency room.

## Common Illnesses We Treat

- Upper respiratory infections
- Flu and cold symptoms
- Sinus infections
- Ear infections
- Strep throat
- Bronchitis
- Allergies
- Urinary tract infections
- Stomach viruses
- Pink eye
- Skin infections

## Our Treatment Approach

1. **Thorough Evaluation**: Our medical professionals will conduct a comprehensive assessment of your symptoms.

2. **Accurate Diagnosis**: We use state-of-the-art diagnostic tools to ensure accurate diagnosis.

3. **Personalized Treatment**: We develop treatment plans tailored to your specific condition and needs.

## When to Visit Us

Visit our urgent care facility when you experience:

- Fever or flu-like symptoms
- Persistent cough
- Sore throat
- Ear pain
- Sinus pressure
- Stomach issues
- Other non-emergency illnesses

## What to Expect

During your visit for illness treatment, you can expect:

- Minimal wait times
- Thorough examination
- Clear explanation of your condition
- Comprehensive treatment plan
- Prescription medications if needed
- Follow-up care instructions

## Insurance and Payment

We work with most major insurance providers and offer affordable self-pay options. Our staff will help you understand your coverage and any associated costs before treatment.
