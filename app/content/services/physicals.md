# Physicals & Wellness at Urgent Care of Twin Falls

We provide comprehensive physical examinations and wellness services to help you maintain your health and meet various requirements for school, sports, or employment.

## Types of Physical Exams

- Sports physicals
- School physicals
- Employment physicals
- DOT physicals
- Annual wellness exams
- Camp physicals
- Pre-participation physicals

## Our Comprehensive Approach

1. **Medical History Review**: We carefully review your medical history and any specific concerns.

2. **Physical Examination**: Our providers conduct a thorough physical assessment including:
   - Vital signs check
   - Height and weight measurements
   - Vision and hearing screening
   - Heart and lung examination
   - Musculoskeletal assessment
   - Reflexes and balance testing

3. **Health Counseling**: We provide personalized health advice and preventive care recommendations.

## Additional Services

- Immunizations and vaccinations
- Health screenings
- Laboratory testing
- Vision and hearing tests
- Blood pressure monitoring
- Diabetes screening
- Cholesterol testing

## What to Bring

Please bring the following to your physical:

- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- Medical history documentation
- Required forms (sports, school, or employment)
- List of current medications
- Immunization records

## Insurance and Payment

We accept most major insurance plans and offer competitive self-pay rates for physical examinations. Contact us to verify coverage and schedule your appointment.
