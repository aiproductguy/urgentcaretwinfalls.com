# Diagnostic Services at Twin Falls Urgent Care

Our state-of-the-art diagnostic services help provide accurate and timely diagnoses for our patients. We offer a comprehensive range of testing options to help identify and treat your medical conditions effectively.

## Available Diagnostic Tests

- **Rapid Strep Testing**
- **Flu Testing**
- **COVID-19 Testing**
- **Urinalysis**
- **Blood Glucose Testing**
- **Pregnancy Testing**

## Why Choose Our Diagnostic Services?

- **Quick Results**: Most tests provide results within minutes
- **Experienced Staff**: Our trained medical professionals ensure accurate testing
- **Modern Equipment**: State-of-the-art diagnostic tools and technology
- **Comprehensive Care**: Testing integrated with treatment plans
- **Convenient Location**: All testing performed on-site

## Insurance and Payment

We accept most major insurance plans and offer competitive self-pay rates for our diagnostic services. Our staff can help verify your coverage and explain any out-of-pocket costs before proceeding with tests.

## When to Seek Diagnostic Testing

Consider visiting us for diagnostic testing if you experience:

- Persistent cold or flu symptoms
- Unexplained pain or discomfort
- Suspected strep throat
- Urinary tract infection symptoms
- Other concerning symptoms requiring diagnosis
