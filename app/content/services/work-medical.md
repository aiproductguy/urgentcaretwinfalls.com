# Work-Related Medical Services at Twin Falls Urgent Care

We provide comprehensive occupational health services and workers' compensation care to support both employers and employees. Our services are designed to promote workplace safety, handle injuries efficiently, and facilitate return-to-work processes.

## Our Services

- **Pre-Employment Services**
  - Physical examinations
  - Drug screening
  - Vision testing
  - Hearing tests
  - Fit-for-duty assessments
  - DOT physicals
  - Pulmonary function testing

- **Workers' Compensation Care**
  - Work-related injury assessment
  - Immediate injury treatment
  - Case management
  - Progress monitoring
  - Return-to-work evaluations
  - Physical therapy referrals
  - Specialist coordination

- **Employer Programs**
  - Custom occupational health programs
  - Drug & alcohol testing programs
  - Random testing management
  - Safety program support

  - Employee wellness initiatives
  - On-site services available
