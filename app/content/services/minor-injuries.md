# Minor Injury Treatment at Twin Falls Urgent Care

When you experience a non-life-threatening injury, our skilled medical team is here to provide prompt, professional care. We treat a wide range of minor injuries with the attention and expertise you deserve.

## Common Injuries We Treat

- **Sprains & Strains**
  - Ankle sprains
  - Wrist injuries
  - Muscle strains
  - Sports-related injuries

- **Cuts & Wounds**
  - Lacerations requiring stitches
  - Minor cuts and scrapes
  - Wound cleaning and dressing
  - Tetanus shots when needed

- **Burns & Abrasions**
  - First-degree burns
  - Minor second-degree burns
  - Road rash
  - Skin abrasions

- **Minor Fractures & Breaks**
  - Simple bone fractures
  - Stress fractures
  - X-ray diagnostics on-site
  - Splinting and casting

## Our Treatment Approach

1. **Quick Assessment**
   - Immediate evaluation of your injury

   - Thorough examination by experienced providers

2. **Advanced Care**
   - State-of-the-art treatment facilities
   - On-site X-ray capabilities
   - Modern wound care techniques
   - Professional splinting and casting

3. **Follow-up Care**
   - Detailed aftercare instructions
   - Wound care guidance
   - Recovery recommendations
   - Follow-up scheduling if needed

## When to Choose Urgent Care

Visit us for minor injuries when:
- You need immediate care but it's not life-threatening
- Your primary care physician isn't available
- You want to avoid expensive emergency room visits
- You need after-hours care for injuries

## When to Seek Emergency Care

Call 911 or visit the emergency room for:
- Severe bleeding that won't stop
- Deep wounds or amputations
- Suspected head, neck, or spine injuries
- Severe burns
- Broken bones protruding through skin
